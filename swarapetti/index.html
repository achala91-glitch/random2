Swara names

Tuning

Natural tuning sets each swara by pure ratios against Sa, as it sounds against a tanpura. Equal tempered matches a keyboard or harmonium.

Register of Sa

Men usually keep Sa in the lower register, often around 1 to 2½ kattai. Choose whatever sits comfortably for the voice or instrument you play along with.

Tanpura first string

Auto tunes it to Pa, or to Ma or Ni for ragams without Pa.

Hints

Progress

Keyboard: 1 to 7 for Sa to Ni, 8 for upper Sa, arrows or WASD for the stick, L to latch it, T to start or stop the talam.
